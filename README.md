A single sentence of horror for the make.fun competition "One Line, One Chill": one line, under 200 characters, that has to frighten a reader in one breath. The dread here comes from a specific, wrong detail rather than gore — a lullaby app that plays a song nobody recorded, at the hour nobody is awake to hear it, and a baby who answers with a sound she is not yet able to make. Nothing is announced and nothing jumps out; the line only gets worse the longer you sit with it, because tonight the app will play again.

## The entry

> The lullaby app had five songs, but at 3 a.m. it played a sixth, a woman humming off-key, and my baby, who cannot hum, hummed back.

(131 characters)

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline.
